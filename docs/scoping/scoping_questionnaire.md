# Scoping Questionnaire — RFP-001: Restaurant Reservation System

**Reference:** RFP-001 · [SOW.md](../SOW.md)

## 1. Scoping Questionnaire

1. How does Fresh Fork currently receive and confirm reservations across the three branches?
2. What are the most common causes of double bookings today?
3. Who will use the system daily: hosts, receptionists, managers, or all of them?
4. What information must be captured for each reservation: customer name, phone, email, party size, notes, occasion, branch, table preference?
5. How should availability be calculated: by number of tables, total seats, fixed time slots, or manual manager approval?
6. Should the MVP support all three branches from day one, or should it start with one branch and expand later?
7. What reservation statuses are needed: pending, confirmed, seated, cancelled, no-show, completed?
8. Does the system need to store repeat customer history in the MVP, or only basic customer contact information?
9. Are there any important restrictions, such as maximum party size, booking time limits, branch opening hours, or peak-hour rules?
10. What would make the MVP successful for Fresh Fork after the first month of use?
